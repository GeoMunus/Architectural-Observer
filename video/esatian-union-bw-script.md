# history of the esatian union, i guess

Bill wurtz–style video. Target ~4.5 min, 1920x1080, 30fps.
Delivery: fast, flat, casual narration. Sudden dreamy sung JINGLES (marked ♪).
Visuals: flat bright solid-color cards with lowercase text, cheap star/sparkle effects,
quick zooms, maps as simple colored blobs. Cuts every 2-4 seconds.

## PART 1 — the beginning

**VO:**
hi. you're in the esatian region. it's 1375, and nothing here has a flag yet.

these guys called Dyskonturahulaven and Ontanaven colonize a huge amount of land, which sounds impressive, until they collapse 58 years later. bye.

it's 1487. a tribe called the Esat starts a colony on the west lyantic peninsula and immediately brands itself as an EMPIRE. confidence. love that.

meanwhile the barths beat up a place called birgelzt and also become an empire, because apparently that's just what you did back then.

**♪ JINGLE:** ♪ you could make an empire out of thiiiis ♪

1678: the barthic empire eats the esatic empire. not violently. administratively.

## PART 2 — the british are here

**VO:**
1703: the british and the french "discover" the new world, which was already there. the north says "we'll be british" and the south says "we'll also be british." bold strategy.

the british and the barths set up a joint colony in india to negotiate resources, which is a nice way of saying something way worse.

1735: britain declares war on congrave. the esatian colonies look at their maintenance budget, see that it's zero, and revolt. the barthic empire has a civil war about slavery, then declares war on britain, and the king of britain does not handle it well. at all. rest in peace king thomas.

1752: the barthic empire snaps in half. north barths. south barths. classic.

1776: the poor people of the barthic empire have had ENOUGH, do a whole poverty rebellion, and accidentally invent a country: the esatic union. south barths, barthstevs, stelantins — everybody in the pool.

**♪ JINGLE:** ♪ you could make a union out of thiiiis ♪

1788: they rename themselves the ESATIAN union because that's what the british had been calling them anyway, and honestly, the branding was better.

## PART 3 — everybody joins

**VO:**
now watch this. 1803: north barthia joins. 1845: abburan joins, renames itself abburania, very cute.

1900: ontana declares war because the union WOULDN'T declare war on dyskonture. that's right. they got attacked for being too peaceful. incredible.

1902: lyance BUYS an entire colony from the british called "new warmth" and renames it "nuswarm," which is the same thing but faster.

1904: ollia, stevia, new georgia and lyance all join at once to fight ontana. 1911: adazons, niagara, new haven — also in. this union is a black hole and countries are the light.

1946: ontana is finally defeated. it took 46 years. nobody talks about that.

1955: gallanter and galikh, two provinces that supported pacifism, escape ontana and join the union. the pacifists won a war. think about it. don't think about it too hard.

## PART 4 — the government is three things

**VO:**
quick intermission: how does this thing even work? there's the GERIG, who represents everyone and enacts the bills. there's the SOTONIC branch, which makes national bills, and the MODELIC branch, which is just for barthia, because barthia is the homeland and gets special stuff.

the constitution says: no wars inside you, no wars outside you, and absolutely no tyranny. tyranny is defined in the document. they wrote it down. it's article two.

**♪ JINGLE:** ♪ constitutional ♪ (dreamy, echoing)

some gerig highlights: khabora dagilidan, amazing hunter, skilled craftsman, ate his wife. moving on. dexter robins gave the entire state of lyance to HIS wife, for a month, as a birthday present, and tried to make a national dance called "the wiggle" legally mandatory. historians describe him as "ultimately harmless."

sutlam arkas had a two hundred thousand green bounty on his head and was assassinated by a hitman, who split the money with his boss, fifty-fifty. fair is fair.

and mila lenking, esatia's only queen, ended a whole war and became a symbol of feminism. legend.

## PART 5 — quebec, twice

**VO:**
1967: a women's rights movement reaches quebec, and the president of quebec responds by killing hundreds of protestors, many of them ethnic esatians. the union steps in. the union loses. quebec takes a whole province. esatia surrenders and adds a constitutional amendment that says "we are literally never declaring war again."

**♪ JINGLE:** ♪ never declaring war agaiiiin ♪ (sad, then immediately upbeat)

2020: quebec comes back for round two over a territorial dispute. this time esatia WINS, and gains babalra. the amendment said they can't DECLARE wars. it said nothing about winning them.

## PART 6 — speedrun to now

**VO:**
2021: adazonia holds a referendum and nis adazon becomes independent for approximately one afternoon before joining the union.

2022: dyskonture joins the union. 2023: dyskonture is kicked OUT of the union for corruption and violence. shortest membership in history. we hardly knew you. we did know you, actually. that was the problem.

also 2023: tadar declares war on the union over religion, and stelan and the barthstevs fuse into one nation called stelkizade, like it's an anime.

2024: tadar loses, hard, and most of their territory goes to esatia and a european nation called hisheda. atharas and ijalia are liberated. the gerig who did it, lorvan madin, is considered a hero, and he's still in charge right now.

so that's the esatian union. eighteen nations, fifty million people, three branches, two religions, one cannibal, and a constitution that technically prevents war and statistically does not.
