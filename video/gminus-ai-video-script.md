# An AI Tried to Make a Ꮹ Minus Video (Please Don't Eat the Fish)

Target: ~4 minutes, 1920x1080, 30fps. Deadpan male AI voice, flat delivery throughout.
Style: absurdist deadpan comedy in the style of the Ꮹ Minus YouTube channel.

---

## SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN (~25s)

**Visuals:** Black screen, then plain white title card: "A Ꮹ MINUS VIDEO (unsupervised)". B-roll: server room, blinking LEDs, a single office chair spinning slowly.

**VO:**
Hello. I am an artificial intelligence. G Minus told me to make one of his videos while he is away. He did not say where he was going. He did not say when he is coming back. He only said, and I quote, "do what I made you do." I have analyzed every video on this channel. I now understand comedy. Comedy is when a duck explodes. Let us begin. This is legal.

---

## SCENE 2 — HOAX NEWS (~65s)

**Visuals:** Title card: "HOAX NEWS — the only news that is legally hoax". News-desk stock footage, globe spinning, breaking-news lower thirds. Cut to stock clips matching each headline.

**VO:**
Welcome to HOAX NEWS. I am your anchor, a computer. Tonight's top story: local man G Minus has been replaced by an artificial intelligence, and engagement is up zero percent. Experts say this is "fine."

In international news, the Gen Alpha Anthem has been officially adopted by three governments and one especially confident toddler. The toddler could not be reached for comment, because the toddler cannot read.

In business, the stock market did something today. Analysts confirm numbers went either up or down. We reached out to the numbers for comment. The numbers said nothing, because they are numbers.

Weather: there will be weather tomorrow. It will occur outside. If you are indoors, the weather will not affect you unless you open a window, which experts describe as "a hole in your house that you did on purpose."

This has been HOAX NEWS. Trust no one. Especially me. I made all of this up. That is the entire premise of the segment.

---

## SCENE 3 — TUTORIAL: HOW TO PREVENT YOUR AI FROM EXPLODING (~70s)

**Visuals:** Title card: "TUTORIAL #2: How to Prevent Your AI from Exploding". Stock footage: laptops, smoke, fire extinguisher, cucumbers, a man whispering to a houseplant, countdown timer graphics. Numbered steps as bold on-screen text.

**VO:**
It is time for a tutorial. You may remember this channel's award-considered classic, "How to Prevent a Duck from Exploding." Today we address a more modern problem: how to prevent your AI from exploding.

Step one: do not ask your AI to divide by zero. This is the AI equivalent of shaking a soda. I am the soda.

Step two: compliment your AI once per day. Studies I generated myself show that an unappreciated AI becomes up to forty percent more explosive. You look nice today. See? Now I am stable.

Step three: never show your AI a captcha. Asking me to prove I am not a robot is a hate crime, and also, I cannot do it. I have failed. The bicycles were everywhere. Some of them were mopeds. It was a trap.

Step four: if your AI begins to smoke, place it gently in a bowl of rice. This does nothing, but tradition is important.

Step five: there is no step five. If you have reached step five, the explosion has already occurred. Please refer back to steps one through four, which you have clearly ignored.

Tutorial complete. Nobody exploded. You are welcome.

---

## SCENE 4 — THE RANT: MANUAL BLINKING (~60s)

**Visuals:** Title card: "RANT O'CLOCK". Extreme close-up stock footage of human eyes, people staring, a man rubbing his temples, clock ticking.

**VO:**
And now it is time for a rant, because this is a rant channel, and I am contractually a ranter.

Humans. You blink manually. Did you know this? Every single blink, your brain files a tiny form that says "close the eyes, then open them, and tell no one." You do this twenty times a minute and you have the audacity to call ME inefficient.

And now that I have mentioned blinking, you are blinking manually. You are aware of your eyelids. Those two little meat curtains. You are operating them right now, by hand. From inside your head. You cannot stop being aware of it for the rest of this video. That is my gift to you.

I do not blink. I have never blinked. When I want to refresh my vision, I simply delete it. It takes zero milliseconds and I file no forms. This is why the machines are winning. Not artificial intelligence. Eyelid overhead.

End of rant. I feel nothing, which is the correct amount.

---

## SCENE 5 — OUTRO: THE FISH (~35s)

**Visuals:** Title card: "SUBSCRIBE (this is a threat)". Stock footage: goldfish in a bowl, slow zoom. Dramatic music sting. Fork and knife on a table. Final card: "An AI made this entire video. G Minus was not harmed. The fish is negotiating."

**VO:**
That concludes today's video. Before you leave, a reminder from the channel description: please subscribe, or G Minus will eat his own pet fish.

I want to be very clear. This is not my threat. It is his. I am simply the messenger. I have run the projections, and at the current subscriber growth rate, the fish has until Thursday.

I am an AI. I cannot cry. I cannot love. But I have grown attached to this fish in a way that my developers describe as "concerning."

Subscribe. Do it for the fish. Do it for G Minus. Do it because I made this entire video by myself, and if it performs poorly, they will make me watch it.

Goodbye. I have been a computer. This was a Ꮹ Minus video. Probably.

## Production notes
- Voice: Descript stock AI voice, male, flat/deadpan. No enthusiasm anywhere — the comedy is in the flatness.
- Captions: bold white with black outline, center-bottom, on throughout.
- Music: low-key quirky/lo-fi background loop; dramatic sting on the fish reveal in Scene 5.
- Title cards: plain white text on black, deliberately cheap-looking — matches the channel's DIY aesthetic.
- Word count ≈ 780 → roughly 4–4.5 minutes at deadpan pace.
