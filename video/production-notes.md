# Production notes — "An AI Tried to Make a Ꮹ Minus Video (Please Don't Eat the Fish)"

An entire video produced autonomously by AI, replicating the experiment from
Dan Dingle's "AI Made This Entire Video by Itself..." — but styled after the
Ꮹ Minus (@gminus) YouTube channel.

## Pipeline
1. Researched the @gminus channel (skits, rants, HOAX NEWS, exploding-duck tutorial, pet-fish subscribe threat).
2. Wrote the full ~780-word deadpan script (`gminus-ai-video-script.md`) — 5 scenes, ~4 minutes.
3. Built the video in Descript via its AI project agent: stock AI deadpan voiceover,
   scene-matched stock b-roll, DIY title cards, bold captions, lo-fi background music,
   dramatic sting on the goldfish reveal.
4. Published from Descript as 1080p video.
